Sequoia NP

6/25/26

The drive in was picture perfect
First hike was Moro Rock. Super steep but very short , great views of the mountains.

Next one I did was the congress loop. I think I'm becoming a Scrooge because I cannot stand the amount of people on the trail. Do they not know that I expected the trail to be completely empty and allow me to enjoy my solitude in nature.  To be fair, once i got deeper into the trail, the crowds disappeared a I was in my happy place. Cool cabins, trees. The end of the loop was the General Sherman Tree.

The largest tree in the world but I’m putting a far asterisk next to that because it’s largest by volume.  Didn’t realize this was a 7th grade math class.  I was definitely being a certified hater. Nonetheless, it was a blast.

After the large tree propaganda,  I went to the visitor center to get some inside scoops on less crowded hikes. I hit Big Baldy at Kings. Fun, good lookouts.
After That I made my way to the campsite where I had dinner with my uncle and aunt.


6/26/26

Mist falls was the shit! Unbelievable viewpoints of kings canyon and the waterfall was mesmerizing. 8 miles out and back but pretty tame for an unreal waterfall. The drive in was wonderful too, basically takes you things the whole park.

After eating some lunch, I did a small hike to General Grant trail to the third largest tree (once again by volume). But I won’t get into it to not rile myself up. My uncle was cooking up the best meals I’ve ever eaten on these trips so i definitely spent a lot more time in the campground than i had in the past. 


6/27/26

Big hike this morning. Pear Lake in Sequoia. 13 miles, 3K elevation. The first 3 miles accounted for 2K of the elevation but after that, it was pretty smooth sailing. It was lakes and marmots galore. After the initial jump, you walk along the heather and emerald lakes and after a bit of a climb, you get spit out by Pear lake. Pear lake was spectacular. A beautiful lake enclosed in massive peaks. Marmots running buck wild. Super secluded as well, a great spot to stop and relax. Hike back down i took watchtower trail which takes you cliff side with incredible views. 


Around sunset I did the north grove loop in Kings. I was the only one on the trail which was peaceful except that my uncle had gotten into my head that i should always carry bear mace (which i did not). 



Pinnacles NP

8/22/26

Hot as hell out there. It was a family affair this trip!  Had my family, my uncle, and my aunt all out for this one. Not as hike centric as usual due to the heat. We started the morning with a 6 mile route through the balconies and caves. After that, the sun beat our asses, I probably should've adhered to the

Countless warnings about avoiding during the summer. It really is no joke and no great escape from it besides the campground’s pool which  was packed to the gills of families looking to cool down. 

Once it cooled down to a chilly 89 degrees, we did Bear Gulch reservoir, the bear gulch caves, and the rim route. Plenty of pinnacles in view. Lots of cool rock climbing routes b small park but the company made it a memorable one for sure. The campground also had so much wildlife. Deer all over. And when the sun went down, the raccoons were up and about. I saw on Reddit that they were a “well trained army” and that was a spot on comparison. These guys were tanks as well, nearly the size of my aunt and uncle’s catahoula.
