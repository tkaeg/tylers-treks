The drive in was picture perfect
First hike was [Moro Rock](https://www.nps.gov/seki/planyourvisit/moro-rock.htm). Super steep but very short , great views of the mountains.

Next one I did was the [congress loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/congress-trail). I think I'm becoming a Scrooge because I cannot stand the amount of people on the trail. Do they not know that I expected the trail to be completely empty and allow me to enjoy my solitude in nature.  To be fair, once i got deeper into the trail, the crowds disappeared a I was in my happy place. Cool cabins, trees. The end of the loop was the General Sherman Tree.

The largest tree in the world but I’m putting a far asterisk next to that because it’s largest by volume.  Didn’t realize this was a 7th grade math class.  I was definitely being a certified hater. * Nonetheless, it was a blast.

After the large tree propaganda,  I went to the visitor center to get some inside scoops on less crowded hikes. I hit Big Baldy at Kings. Fun, good lookouts.
After That I made my way to the campsite where I had dinner with my uncle and aunt.
