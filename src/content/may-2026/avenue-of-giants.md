I began my departure from Redwoods by taking the scenic route. On my way down, I stopped by for one more hike at [Ten Taypo–Hope Creek Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/ten-taypo-hope-creek-loop-trail) off of Newton Scenic Parkway. Easy hike, nobody around, really beautiful and a nice sendoff to wrap up this adventure.

I made one final stop in Prairie Creek Redwoods to check out the [Big Tree](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/big-tree-via-circle-trail). I confirmed that the Big Tree is in fact Big.

From there I headed down the 101 and took State Route 254 to check out the Avenue of Giants. I didn't even try to stop and take photos so I tried making a timelapse which worked decently until my phone overheated while baking on the windshield. It added extra time but was well worth it. And with that wrapped up the week long adventure. 4 parks down, 5 more to go!
