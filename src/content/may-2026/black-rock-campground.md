The second NP I hit this year was Joshua Tree. I went into the park immediately and did the [Hidden Valley loop](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/hidden-valley--3). A nice time scrambling around on some of the rock formations.

Made camp at Black Rock Campground, located about 35 mins from the park entrance. My friends joined me on this trip which most definitely added to the fun. It's a lot easier to play monopoly deal and a lot less sad drinking a beer when I'm not out there all alone. The campsite was great, places to fill water, nice views of the rolling hills, even a snake found it's way to our site! I was recommended to use a tarp to cover the back of my truck when sleeping so I can lay the tailgate hitch down without worrying about any creepy crawlies joining me in my slumber.Best part is that I no longer have to sleep at an angle when the tailgate was up (im short but not 5 foot short).
