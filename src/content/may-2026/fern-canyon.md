Big day. Made oatmeal and coffee in the morning and drove down the 101. The NP ranger gave me a lot of great recommendations so I tried doing as many as possible. I started by checking out Crescent Beach Overlook and then took Newton Scenic Parkway to James Irvine Trailhead over in Prairie Creek.

[James Irvine to Fern Canyon to Clintonia to Miner's Ridge](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/james-irvine-to-fern-canyon-to-clintonia-to-miners-ridge) hike was a beast. Not hard but long for sure. You start off walking through beautiful behemoths of redwoods and then spits you out over in a little creek canyon? I'm not sure how else to describe it. I think Jurassic Park was filmed there so just imagine velociraptors hiding in the trees. There's some fun little plywood bridges you need to cross to go further in the canyon so your feet do get a bit wet from the creek. I was on the trail by 7 on a Wednesday so the trail was near empty until my way back which made it feel even more surreal.

After the hike I did a couple more stops at High Bluff Overlook and Klamath River Overlook. After some food, I did [Simpson Reed trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/simpson-reed-trail) for one last walk amongst the redwoods.
