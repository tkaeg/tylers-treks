The next day we went into the park and did the [Chasm of Doom](https://spearheadadventure.com/joshua-tree-chasm-of-doom) trail near [Hidden Valley trailhead](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/hidden-valley--3). To say I had a tough time finding the entrance was an understatement, I lead us into multiple crevices that just dead ended. Even now, after doing the hike, there is no shot I could even point anyone within 50 yards of the entrance. But luckily some hikers who were also looking for some doom decided to take the lead after watching us walk in circles for 30 minutes. The hike was epic, more climbing and scrambling than an actual hike. The coffin piece was definitely the scariest part (with a name like that who would've thought). You essentially fit yourself through a one foot high slab for about six feet which eventually leads you to a spot where you can stand up in darkness. I only had two breakfast tacos that morning so I was able to slide on through. The rest of the hike is more climbing up rocks, going through tight crevices, and beautiful views of Hidden Valley. Would highly recommend. Joshua tree has definitely been my favorite so far. Camping alone is fun but being with your pals makes it better. The scrambling out there is also unbelievable, it's truly an adult playground. You see a rock and just start climbing it, can't do that everywhere.
