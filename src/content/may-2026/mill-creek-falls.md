I began the day with the most mediocre dehydrated meal I think I've ever had. I'm rereading my journal from that day and I wrote more about this meal than I wrote about all the other things I did that day so it's worth highlighting. I think it was referred to as granola and berries. The directions said to add some warm water and dig in. I guess I was anticipating a more oatmeal taste to it? Idk, it disturbed me to my core. Probably could've just been the fact that it was dehydrated milk that threw me through a loop. Regardless, I was able to percolate my own coffee successfully so that made up for it.

With a stomach full of the best coffee ever and whatever crime you would call wet milk granola soup, I was bound towards the [Mill Creek Falls](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/mill-creek-falls-trail). This one was awesome. With it being so early in the season, there was nobody on the trail and the deer were taking full advantage. I've never seen so many in my life and them being so close to the trail. The waterfall was amazing. Big and loud. I had to capture this hike in videos, the trail and nature around it was full of life and movement. Super easy hike with a great waterfall at the end.

Knowing that the main highway to get around was closed to cars, I rented a bike down at [Highlands Ranch Resort](https://highlandsranchresort.com/), very very nice people there if you're looking to rent a bike for the day. I then biked highway 89 about 5 miles up. It was pretty surreal being the only one on the highway and getting to see the views. The ride up was tough but doable, I stopped for a little salad bag about halfway. I made it up to Lake Helen to check out Lassen Peak then road back down the windy road.

Overall, I liked Lassen Volcanic but I definitely think I went too early. Being able to bike the empty highway was definitely a once in a lifetime experience for me but I bet later in the summer, this place is a blast. Hiking, fishing, river rafting, all sorts of fun to be had.
