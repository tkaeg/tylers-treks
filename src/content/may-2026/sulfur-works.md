Drove up to Lassen to begin the big trip! This trip I was going to hit the 2 most northern parks from me, Redwoods and Lassen Volcanic. Once I got there, I went into the park and checked out Sulfur works. It didn't do much for me, a bit smelly for my liking. The surrounding area was beautiful though. Unfortunately the main highway through the park was closed to cars which made exploring the greater part a bit more manual than I had expected.

I stayed at a campsite in [Mill Creek Resort](https://www.millcreekresort.net/) that was about 20 mins from the southwest entrance. On the way up I acquired a tent from REI for my camper, no more tarp for this guy! It was indeed a battle to setup for the first time. After an hour of hard work, reading and rereading directions, and  cursing the name of the person who recommended I buy this, it was complete. And let me tell ya, it was worth the profanities. Goodbye claustrophobia! Legs able to be kicked out all the way, able to store my valuables in the tent area nearby, enough room for a litterbox for the eventual cat I will adopt to join me on these treks. And my reward to top it all off? Some bbq ribs.
