Last people on the ferry. About a 3 hour trip to Santa Rosa. Saw flying fish and common dolphins. After a quick debriefing from the ranger, we headed towards [cherry canyon trail](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/cherry-canyon-loop). Picturesque views near the top(I think it was the top?) of the coasts of the island. Half the canyon was still closed due to the fires so we audibled down a random trail that eventually got us to [water canyon beach](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Water+Canyon+Beach+Santa+Rosa+Island). Desolate, uninhabited, quiet, beautiful. Blue waters, white sand, if this wasn’t a national park, it would be an all inclusive beach hotel location. Just sippin mai Tais having an entire beach all to yourself under some cliffs that provided some much needed shade. It felt like the Caribbean except the freezing water, which was quite refreshing during the hot day. Ate some Trader Joe’s wraps and jumped in the water and walked along the beach to the end of the walkable coast. On that walk, saw 3 dead seagulls. This was on about a mile of beach. Not only that, the birds were full blown decapitated. Very gnarly. I would’ve taken a pic but I thought it would be inappropriate after the first and second. But then the third came and then I thought “I probably should take a photo of this cuz this is insane” but I held back and waited for a fourth. Lucky for the sensitive stomach people, the fourth one did not come. But how weird is that? 3 decapitated seagulls in under a mile in the middle of a desolate beach?

On the return ferry home, we stopped by the [Painted cave](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Painted+Cave+Santa+Cruz+Island), the 4th largest sea cave in the world (this can be by volume and im okay with that.). Our whole damn boat fit inside! Unbelievable. That alone made the trip worth it. To cap it off, on the way back we saw another pod of dolphins.

Overall, I really enjoyed Santa Rosa. Since there's so many islands, I would definitely go back to try out some of the other ones. Camping would be a blast as well. 

This trip marks my completion of all 9 national parks in California. I’m happy that I achieved my 2026 goal but I’m more thankful that we have access to some of the most beautiful and scenic places on the planet. Each one of them is unique and amazed me in a different way. For 2027, not sure what’s next for the treks but I know I’ll be buying a thesaurus as my vocabulary is on its last legs.
