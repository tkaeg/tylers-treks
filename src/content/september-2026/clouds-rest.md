The big hike today! After 2 failed half dome lotteries, we decided to do [clouds rest](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/clouds-rest-trail). About 13 miles, 3K elevation. All day hike but the views and the lakes were worth it. The “scramble” was very overhyped but the view was not. You can scramble on the sides that are legit cliff drop offs straight into the valley but they aren’t as sketchy as that sounds. You can also just walk down the middle if pretending to be Alex Honnold isn’t your cup of tea. We had parked near the parking lot of the [Sunrise lakes Trailhead](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/sunrise-lakes-trail) so on the way back we walked another tenth of a mile to Tenaya lake. Definitely a must do. You finish a grueling 13 miles and then you treat yourself to a nice dip in a lake that is so beautiful it looks fake. Plus it makes the walk back less agonizing knowing that you’re walking to a gorgeous lake instead of a car that’s been baking in the sun for 4 hours.

Ended the evening with making some steaks and playing more monopoly deal.
