Drove up to Yosemite Southgate hotel on Wednesday and woke up bright and early to the park.  Started the trip off with [glacier point](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/glacierpoint.htm) to [sentinel dome](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/sentinel-dome-trail) to [Taft point](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/taft-point-and-the-fissures-trail) with. Views of half dome and el cap really smack you in the face with how gigantic these mountains are and makes you ask yourself “how is it humanly possible for someone to climb that?”. It was a great way of getting the views of the size of these behemoths before going into the valley. It ended up being around a 7 mile trip, not terribly crowded. Stayed up in upper pines tonight and was able to enjoy the little river that was running next to the campsite
