Little background on this adventure. My buddy and I planned on doing Rim to Rim in Grand Canyon. Had the day locked in, campsite planned, shuttles booked ready to go. About 3 weeks before, a nasty flood ripped apart Phantom Ranch area, effectively closing the North Rim and Phantom Ranch down indefinitely. To make matters worse, all water in the inner canyon was turned off. So obviously we audibled. South Rim to river and back was the new goal. I trained by listening to 12 hours of Over the Edge: Death at the Grand Canyon so i thought i had a pretty good idea of the precautions i needed to take. Basically carry as much water as humanly possible along with a water filter. What i didn't realize was 7 liters of water would end up making my bag weigh 45 pounds.
