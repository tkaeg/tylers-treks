Left Mather at 2am on the dot. Took us about 6 hours to make it down to Pipe Creek Beach. Hiking down [South Kaibab](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/custom-routes/mather-campground-south-kaibab-tonto-trail-bright-angel-to-pipe-creek-back-to-bright-angel-trailhead-37b2985) during the night was an experience like no other. Felt sketchy at times with the dark, steep switchbacks but brought poles for the first time in my life which made a huge difference. The photos I got in this part of the hike were surreal once I figured out the night mode photo setting on iPhone. Photos 1-4 in the slideshow for South Kaibab was pre discovery of this feature. Had to post them to hold myself accountable for how bad these photos were and they made me laugh. Just me using my flash in the pitch black thinking the photos would win a Pulitzer for capturing the raw beauty of the Grand Canyon. Didn't see anyone for the first 4 hours, it was pretty surreal being what felt like the only people in the Grand Canyon. Even with night mode, the photos don't do this place justice. The height of the canyon walls is terrifyingly beautiful. 13 miles down to Pipe Creek. Took a rest at Pipe Creek Beach and had a sandwich before doing the actual hellstorm of going back up 4000 feet.

I used Claude for most of my planning which I have to say was extremely helpful in prep and expectations. However, Claude obviously didn't account for me having that dog in me. Finished 3 hours faster than expected.  It was as bad as expected. Last 4 miles nearly 4K elevation. Never ending. Usually, you're able to be smug as you march towards the finish but all these day hikers thought we were doing the same as them as the [Bright Angel Trail](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/custom-routes/mather-campground-south-kaibab-tonto-trail-bright-angel-to-pipe-creek-back-to-bright-angel-trailhead-37b2985) has plenty of stopping points and turnarounds. One guy had the audacity to say "you aren't that close, it's still like 20 minutes". I've been hiking for 20 miles, 20 minutes is basically there.

We also must've looked pretty awful getting to the top because 3 people from Europe asked us all about our hike and they gave us a nice applause (which made this whole trip completely worth it in my opinion).

We honestly nailed the sun timing. We spent only a couple miles in the direct sunlight but for the most part were in shade. Doesn't mean it wasn't hot as hell. Around 94 degrees in the canyon when we began to go back up. Will without a doubt visit Grand Canyon again, hopefully next time to do Rim to Rim!
