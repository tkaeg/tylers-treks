Little background on this adventure. My buddy and I planned on doing Rim to Rim in Grand Canyon. Had the day locked in, campsite planned, shuttles booked ready to go. About 3 weeks before, a nasty flood ripped apart phantom ranch area, effectively closing the north rim and phantom ranch down indefinitely. To make matters worse, all water in the inner canyon was turned off. So obviously we audibled. South rim to river and back was the new goal. There were many warnings and signs about how it's highly discouraged to do it in one day. Unfortunately, it said discouraged and not illegal and i also listened to 12 hours of Over the Edge: Death at the Grand Canyon so i thought i had a pretty good idea of the precautions i needed to take. Basically carry as much water as humanly possible along with a water filter. What i didn't realize was 7 liters of water would end up making my bag weigh 45 pounds.

Left Mather at 2am on the dot. Took us about 6 hours to make it down to pipe creek beach. Hiking down [south kaibab](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/custom-routes/mather-campground-south-kaibab-tonto-trail-bright-angel-to-pipe-creek-back-to-bright-angel-trailhead-37b2985) during the night was an experience like no other. Felt sketchy at times but brought poles for the first time in my life which made a huge difference. Didn't see anyone for the first 4 hours, it was pretty surreal being what felt like the only people in the Grand Canyon. Photos don't do this place justice. 13 miles down to pipe creek. Taking a rest at pipe creek beach and hitting a sandwich before doing the actual hellstorm and going back up 4000 feet

Claude obviously didn't account for me having that dog in me. Finished 3 hours faster than expected. And we're afraid of AI taking our jobs lol. Shit was hell. Last 4 miles nearly 4K elevation. Never ending. Usually, you're able to be smug as you march towards the finish but all these day hikers thought we were doing the same as them as the [bright angel trail](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/custom-routes/mather-campground-south-kaibab-tonto-trail-bright-angel-to-pipe-creek-back-to-bright-angel-trailhead-37b2985) has plenty of stopping points. One guy had the audacity to say "you aren't that close, it's still like 20 minutes". Hey grandpa, I've been hiking for 20 miles, 20 minutes is basically there.

We also must've looked pretty awful getting to the top because 3 people from Germany asked us all about our hike and they gave us a nice applause like we ran a marathon (arguably this is more impressive).

We honestly nailed the sun timing. We spent only a couple miles in the direct sunlight but for the most part were in shade. Doesn't mean it wasn't hot as all hell. Around 94 degrees when we began to go back up.
