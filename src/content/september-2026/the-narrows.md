Took us about 4.5 hours to get from south rim to Zion. Gorgeous, scenic drive in. Basically at the canyon floor after driving through the longest tunnel I've ever seen. Scrambled to get to the Zion bus which was a disaster in itself. Ended up paying 40 bucks for parking which isn't bad if we weren't arriving at 3pm. We sprinted to the bus. Had a snafu because i didn't have my pass on me. Which actually is something that I am going to throw a fit about. We're running behind already and i get to the entrance and the attendant says they needs my pass. I've got a picture of it but that was just unaccepatable for them. After running back to my car and retrieving it. They do not scan it or anything, just literally looks at the name and my license to make sure they match. Why would a photo not work? Why is there not an electronic system that actually works (I've tried doing it and it's a system created by cave men). Why can't they just look my name up with my license? These are the questions I pondered in the bus ride up to the narrows. I also hadn't eaten anything all day so I may have been more hangry than usual.

[Narrows](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/utah/zion-narrows-trail-to-imlay-temple-and-big-spring) was excellent. Crowded but that was to be expected. Lot deeper of water than planned but i was seeking it out. About waist deep at times. The slot canyon was beautiful. Surrounded by the giant walls. Once we got about 2 miles in, the crowds died down and felt like it was the beginning of the movie The Decent. Floating rock didn't really do it for me but the walls and sandstone were gorgeous. Saw a couple people canyoneering and repelling down the walls which looked like a epic time. Inspired me to dig into more canyoneering, looks like an unbelievable way to see more of these beautiful parks. Walking through the water was a blast but without the rented shoes, it would've been brutal. My knee was ready to explode after rim to river the day before. Will definitely be returning, the park is beautiful and very accessible. Even just driving through the park would've made it worth it.

Would love to come back here. The campsites looked awesome and renting a bike seemed like a fun way to get around town. The little village looked like a lot of fun as well.
