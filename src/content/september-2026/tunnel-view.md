On the way home, we did one final stop right outside the tunnel to get one last view of the valley. It does make sense why people rank it the best NP, it’s gorgeous. We didn’t get the great flowing waterfalls but that’s just another reason to come back. It really is a place everyone should visit at least once in their lives. My only complaint is the Half Dome lottery, I’m convinced it’s fake. There is just no shot in hell that anyone has ever gotten to do Half Dome from the lottery on a Friday or Saturday. I’d be a lot more up in arms about the 10-dollar fee if it wasn’t going to NPS so at least my donation is going to a good cause. Nonetheless, if you show me a person that won the Half Dome lottery on a weekend then you’re showing me a liar.
