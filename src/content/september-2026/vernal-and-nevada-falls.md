[Vernal and Nevada Falls](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/california/mist-trail-to-vernal-and-nevada-falls) was the hike for the day. Really really busy which makes sense as the waterfalls are a sight to see. They were some of the few waterfalls actually running since the snowfall this year was pretty weak.

After the hike, went and relaxed at Sentinel Beach. Perfect way to cool down as the heat in the valley was pretty good. Actual sand in the river too! Not the mystery mush I would’ve expected to find. Departed from the valley to Crane Flat, made some burgers and played Monopoly Deal.
